The welcome treat

Every stay starts with warm monkey bread.

One of your hosts loves to bake. So when you arrive, at any of our six places, there is homemade monkey bread waiting, often still warm from the oven. Some seasons it comes with another dessert, or even homemade ice cream.

It is the thing guests write about most.

Mini Highland cows, mini pigs and fainting goats live in the stables next to our two farm stays, and they love visitors. Treats for feeding are provided. Staying in town? Ask us about stopping by to say hello.

Good to know

Check-in and checkout
Check in from 3:00 PM, check out by 11:00 AM. Self check-in with a smart lock.
Minimum stay
Two nights. Longer stays are welcome, with weekly and monthly discounts.
Home base, not the after-party
Only registered guests stay overnight, and there are no gatherings, tailgates or watch parties. Want a friend to swing by? Just message us first.
Cameras
Exterior security cameras cover the entrances. There are none inside.
Pets
Welcome at The Garage, The Bunkhouse, The Studio and The Cottage.

Level 2 chargers at The Garage, The Bunkhouse, The Midnight Nook and The Cosmopolitan. Public chargers are scarce in Pullman.
